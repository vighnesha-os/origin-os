| Result | Five loader runs in three boots. In every run the FCH's checks passed (an AMD processor; the DSDT's 256-byte region at `0xfed80300`; the firmware's page tables mapping it to itself), and the registers read `PMx00 0xe3070bf7, PMx08 0x021c0514, PMx60 0x0800, PMx74 0x1e060117, PMxD2 0x40`: `0xCF9` decoded, `PMxD2` bit 6 set, the shutdown message and a shutdown as a PCI reset both set. Only `PMxC0` changed. **Boot 1**, one run: `PMxC0 0x00080800` (bit 19, a `0x06` to `0xCF9`) after the provider's own system restarted the machine, with no control in the boot, so the run made the call: `X`, then nothing for 7 min 38 s. **Boots 2 and 3**, alike, two runs each. The first run read `PMxC0 0x00200800` (bit 21, a reset from a sleep state, after the tool's restart) and was the control: `0x06` to `0xCF9`, and a new firmware start followed 6.6 s (boot 2) and 7.1 s (boot 3) after its line, with no line saying the write had not reset. The second run read bit 19, made the call, and printed `X`; nothing followed within the boot: no `Y` (a retry), and no reset, neither the `0xCF9` write due first after a successful return nor the triple fault due 100 ms later. Boot 2 ended at the tool's restart 6 min 34 s after `X`; boot 3 ran until the rental ended, 6 min 9 s after `X`. No verdict |
