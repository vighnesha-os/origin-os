| F1 | Medium | **The NFIT's pairwise checks scale with the whole table, not with `MAX_STRUCTURES`.** `parse` refuses a structure at index 1,024 or later, but each of the first 1,024 structures' `check_references` calls `any`, and `any` walks and decodes **every** structure in the table. ## Checked and found sound

By reading, unless marked:

- **No panic path in the crate's code.** `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`, no dependencies, and Clippy's no-panic lints (indexing, arithmetic, truncating casts, `unwrap`, `expect`, `panic`, `unreachable`) denied outside tests; every read goes through `Bytes`, which bounds-checks with `get` and checked or saturating offsets. The two remaining slice adapters are safe (`chunks_exact(width.max(1))`, `as_chunks`). The no-panic object check's result (4 allowed symbols) is the author's.
- **No slice outside a table.** Every `Bytes`, `Raw` and `Seen` slice is a sub-slice of the bytes the caller gave; a nested structure keeps its table offsets.
- **No unbounded loop.** The walk refuses a length below its type and length fields (zero among them), so every step advances at least 2 bytes, and stops for good at its first error. Counted loops (MPST nodes, PMTT devices, NHLT endpoints and formats) either advance by at least 12 bytes or fail. PMTT recursion is capped at 8, PPTT parent chains at 16 and cache chains at 8, so cycles end as `Cycle`. `Index::at` is bounded by its stride. `PerformanceTable::records`' zero-tail scan stops at the first non-zero byte, so it stays linear.
- **References land on structure starts of the right kind:** PPTT parents (non-leaf nodes), private resources (never a node), next-level caches (caches), offsets into the header refused; VIOT outputs (an IOMMU node); NFIT indexes by type; MPST power-state indexes against the characteristics count.
- **Layouts checked against ACPI 6.6, field by field:** the FADT (every offset from 36 to 268, the length rules, the hardware-reduced ignore ranges, and the `X_` preference as §5.2.9's note words it: the 64-bit field when non-zero, the 32-bit one ignored regardless); the FACS (Table 5.13); the MADT header and types 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 9, 10 and 16 (Tables 5.22 to 5.29, 5.34, 5.35, 5.45) and the INTI encodings (Table 5.26); the SRAT types 0, 1, 2, 5 and 6 and both device handles, the PCI handle's bus in byte 2 and device and function in byte 3 (Tables 5.66 to 5.78); the SLIT (Table 5.81); the SBST, ECDT, CPEP and MSCT (Tables 5.63, 5.64, 5.82 to 5.85), the MSCT's ordering and coverage rules being §5.2.19.1's; the BGRT (Table 5.120); the FPDT's records and the FBPT, S3PT, MBPT and string record (Tables 5.122 to 5.135, the string record's 60-versus-68 noted correctly); the NFIT types 0 to 7 and the interleave combinations (Tables 5.147 to 5.152, 5.149); the HMAT (Tables 5.177 to 5.181, the non-zero base unit and the extended-linear rule being the specification's); the PPTT (Tables 5.189 to 5.192); the PMTT (Tables 5.114 to 5.119: a device's length excludes its children, as the code reads it); the MPST nodes and characteristics (Tables 5.109 to 5.111); the VIOT header and node types 1 to 4 (Tables 5.202 to 5.206).
- **The census maps only through `firmware::bytes`,** with `Access::Read` mappings, refused for memory the memory map calls the kernel's; it writes no firmware memory. The BGRT image and the MP wakeup mailbox are never mapped.
- **It cannot pass while a table is refused.** Every refusal (`report`, `check_facs`, `performance_tables`) increments `refused`, and `consistent` requires `refused == 0`, no unreadable XSDT entry, a checked FADT and DSDT, and a FACS unless hardware-reduced. A DSDT reached under another signature is refused. "ACPI tables" is `Report` in `bootproof.rs`; `: acpi tables failed` is among xtask's failure words, so the in-order markers (which match loosely within a line) are backed by the verdict.
- **Printing.** Signatures and OEM IDs are printed through `printable`/`Ascii`, which replace anything but printable ASCII; no firmware-sized string is printed.
- **Gates.** The crate is in `FREESTANDING_LIBS` (Clippy on the host and both freestanding targets, the no-panic object check), in `KERNEL_LEAVES`, and in `LAYERS` as `("origin-acpi-tables", &[])`, with `framekernel` the only crate above it; its manifest has no dependencies, so it is a leaf with nothing below. `origin-frame` does not depend on it.
- **IOMMU ordering.** The census runs after `iommu::init` has recorded every writable identity range, so its lending cannot change which ranges the IOMMU accepts; an overlap makes a table "not readable" instead.

Inferred and not verified: that QEMU's tables are as the hand-over describes, and the gates' and boots' results; these are the author's.

## Records that say more than the code shows

- **TASKS item 62, security invariant (3), and the crate's "Bounds on work":** "1,024 NFIT structures" bounds how many structures are checked, not the work, which is up to 1,024 walks of the whole table (F1).
- **TASKS item 62, security invariant (5):** "no mailbox, channel, image or global lock is touched". The census decodes and prints the global lock's Owned bit, through a reference it lends for the kernel's life over memory §5.2.10.1 says firmware updates (F2). It writes nothing, which is what "read-only" there holds.
- **`firmware_bytes`' premise** ("firmware tables do not change after boot") is now applied to the FACS and the S3PT and FBPT, for which §5.2.10.1, §5.2.24.8 and §5.2.24.10 say otherwise (F2).
- **The hand-over's list for step 5** puts "the SLIT's diagonal and reserved distances" among the places "the kernel's own readers never refused". `parse_slit` does not, but the kernel's NUMA distances proof already fails on a diagonal other than 10 or an off-diagonal below 10. For the SLIT, the crate moves the refusal from the proof to the parser and changes no verdict (it accepts zero localities, which the proof fails).
- **The contract** (rulings accepted) says the MADT refuses overlapping I/O APIC ranges (F17), the SBST checks its levels' order (none is checked; the crate's documentation says why), and every structure iterator stops at its first error (F15). The crate's documentation is accurate on all three; the contract is not.
- **TASKS item 62's required tests and STATUS** describe a hostile soak "drawn towards the boundaries"; its one depth assertion is an aggregate acceptance above 2 %, and no generated table is large (F13).
- **ADR-0013's AT-3** is recorded as the DSDT "looked for under another signature"; that edit is in the kernel's census, not the crate, so no control exercises a rule of the crate's FADT. The record says the `X_` preference rests on a host test, which is accurate.
