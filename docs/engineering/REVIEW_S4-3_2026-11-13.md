## Checked and found sound

- **The fix's order** **[code]**. `ready_records` refuses a run over a mapped page first, at any length, then a run with `len + count > bound`, then reserves room. `prepare_run` maps the three to `MapError::AlreadyMapped`, `ChangeError::Limit` and `MapError::OutOfMemory`. That is the order and the statuses the second note on D5 and the note on D4 ask for.
- **The fix leaves nothing behind.** A refused run changes neither table's length nor capacity, and `tables.refused` is reset only after the check (the stale value is never read before it is reset) **[code]**. It refuses nothing that could have been mapped: each record is a present leaf, each leaf table maps 512 pages, and 32 tables with a PML4, a PDPT and a PD leave at most 29 leaf tables **[code + inferred]**.
- **The bound is derived, not written.** `MAX_USER_PAGES = (MAX_TABLES - (LEVELS - 1)) * ENTRIES`, with `LEVELS` from `USER_END + PAGE` (bit 47), `PAGE` and `ENTRIES`: 4 levels and 14,848 pages **[code; arithmetic checked]**. Only the host tests' `29 * 512` is written by hand.
- **`bbc15f5`.** Each new constant comes from `size_of` and `align_of` of the type the allocation uses: `Counted<T>` as in `try_new`, `[T; 1]` as `Node::try_new` boxes it, `Mapping`, `Slot<K>` **[code]**. The diff adds no `unsafe` line and changes none **[code]**.
- **The purse** **[code]**. `pay` is one `try_update` with `checked_sub`, so a payment and its check are one step and the balance cannot go below zero. `Payment` is neither `Clone` nor `Copy`. Its drop swaps its bytes to zero before adding them back. `add` moves a new payment's bytes into the old one and drops the empty one. `give_back` gives at most what it holds. `size_once` publishes `left` last, so a racing payment finds nothing. A refund racing a payment only retries the payment's compare-and-swap. The balance never passes the size, since only what was taken comes back.
- **Paid before made** **[code]**. Every constructor of `Process` goes through `with_origin`: `proof`, `proof_child_of` and `pinned_child_of`. It computes `costs()` and the figure, pays `figure + ahead` with checked addition, and only then makes the space and the account. It draws the ID last. Each failure after the payment drops the payment where it is.
- **A launch's pages** **[code]**. `map_page` and `map_core` pay before they map, out of what was paid ahead or out of the root. A failed map gives back exactly what it took, where it took it from. `settle_launch` swaps `ahead` to 0, so it is idempotent. `launch_ahead` counts whole pages, and `program::check` requires each segment's size to be a page multiple. The record is counted at `start::MAX_SIZE`, rounded up. A launch that fails after the payment drops the child, and with it the whole payment.
- **Arithmetic** **[code]**. Every product of a limit value is checked: `times`, `growing`, `footprint`, the frames of `committed`, `Figure::total`, `launch_ahead`, `with_origin`'s sum. Overflow gives no figure and `limit`. Zero gives 0 for every kind but the process's fixed part. The proof's subtractions are each reached only after a payment that proves them safe.
- **Each kind's allocations, as read** **[code]**. A channel: its block, two queues and two charge lists of 16, each from `try_reserve_exact`. A close moves a queue to `undelivered` without allocating, and `reap_closed` runs at the end of every system call, so O11's "within the same call" holds. A memory object: its block, an exact list of 8-byte `SharedFrame`s (at most 16 bytes a page once rounded), a `SharedInner` and a frame a page; its length must be a non-zero multiple of a page. A mapping: `Mappings::reserve` checks the limit before `try_reserve(1)`. Page records: both tables grow only to `len + count <= bound` or `len + 1`, and `retire` never grows the taken spare. An event, a port: one block each; a port's queue is intrusive. A binding: two one-element nodes, and the index share (K7 aside). A thread: a 16 KiB stack from the region (whose page tables are made at boot), an FPU area from `Layout(size, 64)` with the same size formula as `costs`, `ThreadObject` and `UserContext`. The run queue and zombie slots are fixed arrays. The handle table's two lists never need more than `limit` while no slot is retired (B7).
- **`growing`'s bound** **[code + inferred]**. A `Vec` grows only when what it needs passes its capacity, to the larger of twice that capacity and what it needs. Every caller here needs at most `most`, so the old capacity is at most `most - 1`, the new one at most `max(4, most, 2 * (most - 1))`, and both blocks are counted. This holds for any history of growth and shrinkage, and for `spare.try_reserve(total)` on an empty `spare`.
- **The threads count** **[code]**. It is checked under the lifecycle lock that a start already takes, before the context or the stack is made. `start_failed` and `thread_reaped` decrement it. The `ThreadObject` made before the check is freed on refusal.
- **The process's own end** **[code]**. `reap_zombies` frees the FPU area, gives the stack back, drops the `UserContext` (the space, when it is the last reference) and calls `thread_reaped`, and only then drops its clone of the process. `Process` drops `space` before `account`. `MemoryObject` drops `frames` before `_charge`. `KernelChannel` drops `account` last.
- **No default** **[code]**. `Limits` derives no `Default` and has no constructor. Every literal in the diff names `threads`, or is built from a named value with `..`.
- **The O2 test** **[code]**. `handles_in` matches every `Type` variant with no wildcard, so a new variant fails the build. Results are walked through structs, arrays and vectors. Any handle result but `handle_duplicate`'s fails, and the count of methods (20) and the version (0.7) are pinned.
- **xtask** **[code]**. The new failure word is in `boot_failures`. The markers are required in both the generator and the no-generator scenarios, the latter with "not run, no generator". `kernel_bound.rs` is in the names gate's list with its reason.
- **The root's sizing** **[code]**. It is sized after the loader's memory is reclaimed and `origin.core` is installed, and before `sched::prove`. It prints the root and the reserve. A second sizing is refused and checked twice: once at boot, once in the proof.

## Resolution

Written by the author, the S4-3 session, on `claude/elegant-benz-1983ff`. Nothing above this section is changed but the "Status" column. The coordinating session ruled on the four questions, none for the owner: (1) count B1, do not reorder; (2) reorder for B2, the coordinator's to rule; (3) B19's model names everything the reserve takes; (4) B3, B4 and B10 before landing, each new clause with its control.
