## Checked and found sound

By reading, unless marked:

- **Unit arithmetic.** The first and last unit indices, the clip of each unit to the field (`lo`, `hi`, `n`, `shift`), the placement of read bits with `get_bits`/`set_bits`, and the write composition `(base & !mask) | (field & mask)` are right for every width; a write covering a whole unit skips the read; Preserve reads first, WriteAsOnes and WriteAsZeros fill. Every unit is checked inside its region (`byte_off + w ≤ length`) with checked address arithmetic. `AnyAcc` takes the narrowest naturally aligned width holding the field, else bytes (G5); EmbeddedControl and SystemCMOS take bytes.
- **Index and bank fields.** The index register is written with the unit's byte offset (aligned to the access width), then the data register read or written; Preserve reads both first; a bank field writes its bank value before every access; chains stop at `FIELD_DEPTH`.
- **Values.** Integers are truncated to the field, buffers zero-extended, a buffer larger than the field written in pieces, lower first (Table 19.7); a field no wider than an integer reads as one; a buffer-protocol write returns the host's buffer as `Store`'s result.
- **Time.** Every read, write and transfer carries the time left and none is made with none; each unit is charged as a step; the Global Lock is never asked for with 0xFFFF and a wait is cut to the time left; `Sleep` and `Stall` are charged before they run; `Stall` takes a byte (G7).
- **The Global Lock on error paths.** A `Lock` field's lock is given back on every exit from `field_at`, error paths included; `\_GL` taken by `Acquire` is released by `Release`, by `abort` and at the end of an evaluation (but B2-5 and B2-6).
- **Mutexes and events.** Nesting by depth, sync levels on acquire and release (G2), a serialized method's implicit mutex at its level and dropped at its return, held mutexes as collector roots; `Signal`, `Reset` and `Wait` count and time out as §8 says.
- **`call_nested`.** On error it pops the frames, activations and creators it added; on every path it restores the base, result, cursor and depth; the collector is held off while it runs (`nested`).
- **The PCI walk** is right for a region declared in a device under a chain of bridge devices (secondary buses read from the root down), in the root bridge's own scope (its `_ADR`, bus `_BBN`), and with no root bridge above (segment 0, bus 0, the device's `_ADR`), as the author's test shows for the first two.
- **`Notify`** goes to devices, processors and thermal zones only; **`Fatal`** is reported, then ends the evaluation; **`Debug`** gives text; **`BreakPoint`** calls the host.
- **`_OSI`, `_OS`, `_REV`** match ADR-0019 Amendment 1 on `main`: the 22 strings are exactly Microsoft's table, in its order and spelling (page dated 2024-09-11, read 2026-11-14), compared byte for byte; Ones is the width's mask (0xFFFFFFFF under a revision-1 DSDT, as Microsoft documents); everything else is Zero, `Linux` and the feature-group strings included; `_REV` is 2; `_OS` is the provisional "Origin OS". The table is one `const`.
- **`initialize()`** runs `\_SB._INI` first, then Table 6.67's three cases with `_STA` absent meaning 0x0F; a failing `_STA` or `_INI` is reported and the walk goes on; it never descends into a method and walks by links, not recursion.
- **`connect_space()`** finds the scopes in one linear pass, each once, in definition order; it runs nothing for SystemMemory and SystemIO, nor for PCI_Config regions in a scope with `_BBN`, as the ledger reads §6.5.4.
- **No panic site** in `region.rs`, `sync.rs` or `init.rs`: every sum, shift and conversion is checked or saturating, and every loop is bounded by its field, list or the namespace.

## Records that say more than the code shows

- **`engine.rs` `call_nested`**: "Used where an operator needs a method's answer at once". That is Rust recursion, which the contract's §4 rules out (B2-1).
- **`region.rs`'s module text**: a PCI_Config region names "the function's `_ADR`". It is the `_ADR` of the region's parent node, which may be a method or another scope (B2-2).
- **`host.rs` `RegionAccess::region`**: "The region's path from the root". Empty for the bridge reads the library makes itself (B2-3).
- **`sync.rs`'s module text**: "an evaluation that ends holding a mutex has every one released and reports `MutexHeld`". Not for what export takes (B2-6).
- **`init.rs`'s module text**: "Each `_STA`, `_INI` and `_REG` is an evaluation of its own, with its own limits. The walk itself allocates nothing". True, and the walk as a whole is bounded by nothing (B2-7).
- **`host.rs`'s module text**: "[`Host::table`] by none yet". `DataTableRegion` calls it.
- **`host.rs`**: "No call waits past the time limit". True of the calls that wait; the others are not constrained (B2-12).
- **ADR-0019 Amendment 1 on `main`**: "Not built: TASKS item 63 builds it in its step B2". `_OSI` and `_REV` were built in B1; B2 left them as they were.

## Resolution

Written by the author, the TASKS 63 session, on `claude/aml-interpreter`. Nothing above this section is changed but the "Status" column. The coordinating session ruled on each finding. Before the answers the branch merged the fifth batch (`5a7569b3`); each answer is a commit of its own, after the answers to the B1 addendum and apart from them. No person has reviewed the answers, and the reviewer has not yet read them.

**B2-1 (High): fixed, `6893a190`.** A PCI_Config region's function is found by frames on the engine's own stack. A field read or write first looks through the field and its bank and index registers for a PCI_Config region whose function is not known (`unresolved`, bounded by the chain's depth); if there is one, nothing has reached the host, the step is put back as it was (its frame, the activations and creators it added, the cursor, the depth) and a `Resolve` frame is pushed above it (`pci.rs`). That frame asks `_HID`, `_CID`, `_SEG`, `_BBN` and `_ADR` in turn; a method among them runs as an ordinary call and hands its value back to the frame, as to an operator. When the function is known it is kept in the region, the frame goes, and the step runs again. A walk that needs the same region's access again is `ErrorKind::AddressCycle`. Outside the frames, a field read as the evaluated name and `export` go through `with_address`, which runs the frames once and tries again, with the collector held off. `call_nested` and `value_of` are gone. Tests, on a thread with a 192 KiB stack under the boot proof's limits: an `_ADR` that reads its own region's field ends with `AddressCycle`, from a method and from the field's own name, with nothing sent to the host; an `_ADR` thirty calls deep gives the function. Control, run once: with the old walk the first test overflows that stack and the process aborts. Follow-up `0051a93b`: the count of `Resolve` frames, which lets the cycle check skip its scan when there is none, now follows their removal by an error.

**B2-2 (High): fixed, `5b5a3a9b`.** The function is the nearest `Device` above the region; going up, only devices are asked whether they are a root bridge; going down, each device between with an `_ADR` is a bridge and one with none is passed over; the function's own `_ADR` is required (`NotFound`, never 0), as is a device above the region. Test: under a root bridge › `RP01` (device 0x1C, secondary bus 5) › `PXSX`, a region in a method of `PXSX` writes `PXSX`'s function after one bridge read, `RP01`'s; one in a power resource of `RP01` writes `RP01`'s; one in a thermal zone inside `PXSX` reads `PXSX`'s; a device with no `_ADR` is refused with nothing sent. Control, run once: with the walk by scopes, the method's region makes two bridge reads. "Under two bridges" is the B1-era test's case, still passing.

**B2-3 (Medium): fixed, `5b5dc477`.** (a) A bridge's secondary bus is `Host::bridge_bus`, a request of its own (`BridgeBus`: the bridge and the time left) with its own trace event, never a region access with an empty path; its failure is `Host(cause)`. (b) A buffer-protocol or GPIO field whose first byte is not below its region's length is refused before anything is sent. Tests: the PCI tests see each bridge through `bridge_bus` and no access at 0x19; an SMBus field at 0x30 of a 0x10-byte region and a GPIO pin at byte 2 of a 1-byte region are `Range` with no transfer. Control, run once: without the length checks the SMBus field is transferred.

**B2-4 (Medium): fixed, `96eb2026`.** Each field unit keeps where it starts in bits from its `Connection` (from the region's start if it has none), and `BufferAccess` carries that (`bit_offset`) and the field's length (`bit_length`). Test: `Connection (CON), A, 1, B, 2, Connection (CO2), C, 4` gives the host pins 0 (one), 1 (two) of CON and 0 (four) of CO2. Control, run once: counted from the region's start, C arrives at pin 3.

**B2-5 (Medium): fixed, `2b43c166`.** The evaluation counts its holds of the Global Lock: the host is asked on the first and released on the last; a release with none held is `NotOwned`. The model host's lock now times out when asked for while held, as a literal FACS lock would. Test: `Acquire (\_GL)` around a `Lock` field, and a `Lock` index field over `Lock` registers, each succeed with one host acquisition, given back. Control, run once: without the count, the first ends with `Limit(Time)`.

**B2-6 (Medium): fixed, `ed0e7bf6`.** After `export`, everything held above the evaluation's mark is released and reported as `MutexHeld`, and a failed export is aborted to the marks like any failure; `end_evaluation` at the top level releases whatever is still held and gives back a Global Lock hold left counted, as a backstop (the planted fault KeepGlobalLock plants there too). Test: a package naming a field below a device whose `_ADR` takes `MX` (level 5) and keeps it: the evaluation reports `MutexHeld`, nothing stays held, a level-1 mutex can be taken afterwards. Control, run once: with the release before export alone, the evaluation succeeded with `MX` kept. Since B2-10 the `_ADR` is stopped at its own return; the test says so and checks the same each time.

**B2-7 (Medium): fixed, `45ba4a3a`.** `initialize()` (which now returns a `Result`) and `connect_space()` count against the limits' time from the walk's start and their steps summed over every evaluation and node; each evaluation gets the less of its own limits and what the walk has left; with nothing left the walk stops and returns the limit. An evaluation's end collects only when the collection is due, by the spacing `tick` uses, and counts afresh with each collection. Tests: 200 devices whose `_STA` sleeps 10 ms, under the boot proof's one second, stop with `Limit(Time)` after 95 to 101 devices (control: all 200 without the bound); 20,100 devices with plain-name `_STA`s are walked in about 1 ms, release (control: 5.4 s with a collection and recount per evaluation). QEMU's walks take 12 to 180 µs, from 0.1 to 2.6 ms. Three tests that measured what is held right after an evaluation now force that collection first.

**B2-8 (Low): fixed, `8d71de80`.** The span of all a field's units is checked against its region before the first is touched. Test: a 16-bit field from byte 3 of a 4-byte region is `Range` with nothing sent. Control, run once: byte 3 was written before the refusal. The second half, a unit wider than its region refused though the field fits, is kept as the strict choice and recorded as G16.

**B2-9 (Low): fixed, `f62b0c68`.** An `_ADR` must name device 0 to 31 and function 0 to 7, nothing above bit 31; anything else, 0xFFFF among it, is `Range`, for the function and each bridge. Test: 0x0001_FFFF, 0x0020_0000 and 0x0000_0008 are refused with nothing sent; 0x001F_0007 reaches device 31, function 7. Control, run once: the first was read as function 255.

**B2-10 (Low): fixed, `e3467197`.** `Acquire` records the activation that took the mutex, and a method's return releases what it still holds and fails with `MutexHeld`, so its caller does not go on; load-time code is released and reported at its evaluation's end.
