## What the reviewer found sound

- **The entry stub's register moves.** The stub saves its arguments before RDMSR overwrites RCX and RDX, and RSI holds the hand-over until `call r10`.
  - `_start` leaves RSI alone, and `Entry` is one transparent pointer, so the value arrives as `kernel_main`'s second argument.
  - Both waits are bounded. Nothing touches the stack between the CR3 load and the stack switch.
  - The stub fits in the two pages mapped at their identity address.
- **The register gating:**
  - PAT is gated on CPUID 01H:EDX[16]. The three shadow-stack registers are gated on CET_SS, and only when leaf 7 exists.
  - `preserves_flags` is claimed only on IN, OUT and RDMSR, not on the moves from CR0 and CR4.
- **Breadcrumbs 1 to 3** do not depend on relocations: no pointer stored in data is read on their path.
- **The scenarios.** The quiet period is real, since QEMU is stopped after it. The ESP is laid out afresh for each scenario, so the switch file cannot carry over. The stale-key indexing matches the one retry `exit_boot_services` allows.
- **The switch's file read** is bounded to 16 bytes, its path is NUL-terminated, and every handle it opens is closed.
